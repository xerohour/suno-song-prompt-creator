📖 SUNO-Bible Quick Guide

1. Style-Mesh Theory

Suno doesn't pick genres discretely. Styles are statistical clusters. Tags like rap ↔ trap ↔ bass ↔ hip hop pull together. Choose tags from the same cloud for coherence, or stack distant genres for uniqueness.

2. Pop Gravity Well

Pop is the ontological center. Everything drifts toward pop unless you exclude it. Rule: If not making pop, add no pop, no polished hooks, raw.

3. Prompt Formula

decade, genre, subgenre, country, vocalist info, style + mood + instruments
Example: 1970s, rock, progressive rock, UK, male British vocals, epic and mystical, lush guitars, analog synths

4. Lyric Tags

Valid base tags: [Intro] [Verse] [Pre-Chorus] [Chorus] [Bridge] [Solo] [Interlude] [Break] [Outro] [End]

  • Modifiers: Add emotion + pace: [Haunting Whispered Pre-Chorus]
  • Breaks: Specify instrument: [Violin Break]
  • Expression: ... slows, ! emphasizes, (parenthesis) = call-response, ooooh = vocalizations
  • Instrumentals: Use . . . ! rhythm patterns under [Interlude]

5. Sacred Strategies

  • Avoid gravity wells: don't use pop, bass, beat, hook, catchy unless essential
  • Force exclusions: "90s hip hop, boom bap, no trap, no modern production"
  • Be concrete: distorted guitars beats nice guitars
